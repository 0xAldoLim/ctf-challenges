# CTF Challenges

This curated archive catalogs CTF challenges for reproducible cybersecurity research, including solution-blind evaluation of PRESTIGE. The original organizers and authors retain credit for their challenges. The archive excludes solutions and reference flags.

Some challenges may need external services or operator infrastructure. Publication depends on the applicable upstream redistribution permissions. A provenance-only entry records origin and attribution without including challenge materials or a runnable package.
