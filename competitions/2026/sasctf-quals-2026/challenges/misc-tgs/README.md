## Title
Behind the Buffet

## Description
I subscribed to this writer's channel ages ago. There weren't even any posts there back then, but this was one of those cases where you don't want to miss a single line from the pen of a great author. Recently, he resurfaced and started writing.

To be honest, I found myself disappointed by how shallow his take on the subject was. I had expected at least a few layers of depth - the kind where meaning reveals itself gradually, one insight after another. I wanted to read something that would make me pause and think. But here we are. Or perhaps I've simply missed the point?
