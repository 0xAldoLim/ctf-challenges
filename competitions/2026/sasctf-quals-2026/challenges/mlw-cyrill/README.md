## Title
First Day First

## Description
Meet Cyril Cyrilov, a newcomer at APC IB International Logistics.

Today is his first day at work, and he is still setting up his machine before diving into the features of the company's huge monorepo. Cyril has big plans, a fresh account, and a long onboarding checklist ahead of him.

But one question remains: will he be able to smoke with his colleagues during the workday, or will he have to do it alone for the rest of his life?
